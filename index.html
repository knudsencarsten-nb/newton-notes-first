Interactive Notes: Newton's Laws

1. Newton's First Law

An object remains at rest or moves with constant velocity unless acted upon by a net external force.

Definition: If the net force on an object is zero, its acceleration is zero.

Example: A hockey puck sliding on nearly frictionless ice keeps moving almost with constant velocity because the horizontal net force is very small.

3. Newton's Third Law

When object A exerts a force on object B, object B exerts an equal and opposite force on object A.

Important: The two forces act on different objects, so they do not cancel each other on a single object.
